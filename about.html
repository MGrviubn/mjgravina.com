About Me

I’m Matthew Gravina, and I’m pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Cybersecurity at Brigham Young University–Idaho after previously attending Modesto Junior College. My interests include computer systems, resin 3D printing, miniatures, and understanding how things work.

I’m a member of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and an Eagle Scout. I served a mission in Tucson, Arizona, which ended early because of the COVID-19 pandemic.

Outside coursework, I enjoy hands-on projects and spending time with my pets. Some of their less dignified moments may also find their way into this portfolio.

What I’m Learning

My current coursework includes networking, web development, electronics, and artificial intelligence. I’m practicing network configuration and troubleshooting, building websites with HTML and CSS, and working with circuits and wireless controllers.

Alongside my classes, I’m learning about 3D modeling in Blender so I can begin creating my own printable designs.

Beyond Coursework
