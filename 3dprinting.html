3D Printing

Draft — this page is still being developed.

I primarily use resin 3D printing to make tabletop models.

Resin Printing

I work with an Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra 16K and Lychee Slicer, handling preparation, printing, cleaning, curing, and assembly myself. The models I currently print come from STL files created by other designers.

I’ve fine-tuned printer settings to produce accurate prints with fine detail. I’ve also worked on model orientation and support placement to reduce printing failures and avoid problems with hollow pieces.

Learning Blender

My next goal is to learn how to create some of my own models in Blender. I’m considering simple objects, such as dice, as a starting point before developing more involved mechanical and character ideas.
