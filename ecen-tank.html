Wireless Tank — In Progress

I’m working toward a wirelessly controlled model tank using a freely available model that resembles a Warhammer 40,000 Baneblade.

So far, I’ve established a wireless connection between my controller badge and an ESP32 receiver. Inputs from the badge appear on a display connected to the receiver. I’m currently printing parts and ordering the other components needed for assembly.

The next goal is to assemble the tank and use the wireless controls to drive it. Physical movement remains a work in progress.

Model credit: Broadsword Superheavy Tank by nfeyma, available on Cults3D.

Motor Circuit Coursework

In a classroom circuit, I checked the connections, repaired a loose motor lead by soldering it, and tested the motor separately. The circuit worked after I reversed the transistor’s orientation.

My teacher explained that the supplied transistor had a different lead order. The exercise gave me practice checking individual parts and connections when a circuit did not work as expected.
